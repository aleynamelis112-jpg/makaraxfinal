# **App Name**: Makara Pati

## Core Features:

- Product Catalog Display: Showcase pet products from images found in the public/images folder, automatically assigning names (or 'Makara Pati Özel Ürün' if not obvious), displaying a fixed '0 TL' price, and including a 'WhatsApp’tan Sipariş Ver' button for each product.
- Responsive Navigation: Implement a sticky top navigation bar featuring the brand logo and 'Makara Pati' text on the left, core menu links (Ana Sayfa, Ürünler, Hakkımızda, İletişim) centrally, and Instagram and WhatsApp buttons on the right, collapsing into a hamburger menu for mobile views.
- Interactive Hero Section: Display a prominent hero area with the main title 'Makara Pati', a descriptive subtitle, a large and modern display of the logo, and two call-to-action buttons for 'Ürünleri İncele' and 'WhatsApp’tan Sipariş Ver'.
- Information Content Pages: Provide dedicated sections for 'Hakkımızda' (About Us) outlining the brand's mission and 'İletişim' (Contact) details including social media links and address, complemented by a persistent footer with copyright information.
- Direct WhatsApp Ordering: Integrate a direct WhatsApp chat link into every product card and hero section, pre-populating the message for product-specific inquiries ('Merhaba, Makara Pati sitesindeki [ÜRÜN ADI] ürününü sipariş vermek istiyorum.') to +90 547 571 71 77.
- Premium Design Elements: Apply a luxurious visual style including glassmorphism effect for product cards, soft shadows, rounded corners, a modern gradient background, and interactive hover effects (subtle growth and glow) on product cards.
- SEO & Performance Basics: Establish a fundamental SEO-friendly structure with proper meta title and description, and ensure all images are optimized for fast loading and uncompromised quality, crucial for search engine visibility and user experience.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant orange (#FF8000) to serve as a high-contrast accent for interactive elements and highlights, exuding energy and modern flair within the dark theme.
- Background color: Deep anthracite (#211E1D), forming the core dark theme. This color provides a sophisticated and premium base for content and components.
- Accent color: Pure white (#FFFFFF) for text, critical UI elements, and stark contrasts, ensuring readability and elegance against the dark background, as specified by the user's palette.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for a modern, objective, and highly legible aesthetic suitable for both titles and body text, supporting a clean and professional appearance across the site.
- Utilize clean, minimalist vector icons for social media links (Instagram, WhatsApp), navigation controls (hamburger menu), and functional elements (like a scroll-to-top button), aligning with the site's modern and premium design.
- Responsive grid layout for product cards (single column on mobile, two on tablet, and three or four on desktop), combined with consistent rounded corners across UI components, and balanced use of negative space for a luxurious, uncluttered presentation.
- Incorporate subtle page opening and scroll animations, smooth scrolling throughout the site, and interactive hover effects on product cards (light scaling and glow) to enhance engagement and provide a polished user experience.
